### Section 8: the hypocrite and the believer; the name and the meaning

Mathnawi 1:285-298. Voice in the text: Rumi, in his own voice. Two half-lines are Qur'an: 'with Him is the Mother of the Book' (13:39) and 'between them is a barrier they do not overpass' (55:20). `urn:sufi:rumi.mathnawi:1.b0285` (Mathnawi 1:285); `urn:sufi:rumi.mathnawi:1.b0298` (Mathnawi 1:298)

- **Claude's analysis** (Claude (the assistant); draft): The first sentence is Rumi's own (298). The rest gives a reason Rumi does not state here. What we hold: sweet and bitter are one at their source and separate in this world; the letter is only a vessel; on Housam's reading, bitterness measures how far a thing has come from its source, and the cure is to travel back.

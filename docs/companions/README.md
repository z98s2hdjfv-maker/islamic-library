# v14: Companions and caliphs canon

58 works from OpenITI, pinned to exact commits and SHA-256 (`catalogs/companions_canon_pins.json`), built by `pipeline/works/ingest_companions_canon.py`. List drafted by Claude as a starting canon; a scholar should review it.

The four caliphs and the Companions left no books of their own. Their rulings and words survive in the collections below, so every attributed saying should be checked against its isnād (group `rijal`) before a council voice quotes it.

Folders under `corpus/` are topic groups (search `--author athar`, `--author sira`, ...). Attribution per work is in `catalogs/works_index.tsv`.

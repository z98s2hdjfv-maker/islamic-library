## Contradicted by the biographers
- Birth 471 and age ninety (al-Dhahabi, `:p125974`, `:p126083`), not 470 and 91. Died 10 Rabiʿ II 561.
- He studied law under the Hanbali Abu Saʿd al-Mukharrimi from his youth (`:p125975`); Ibn Qudama read al-Khiraqi with him (`:p125992`).
- Ibn al-Jawzi: he was given al-Mukharrimi's school and taught and preached there until he died (`:p125990`); the wandering came before the preaching, not in the middle (order from general knowledge).

## Cautions found

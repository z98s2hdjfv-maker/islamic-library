# Knysh–Risāla concordance (v28)

`reports/concordance/knysh_risala.tsv` maps the 164 headings of Alexander Knysh's translation, *Al-Qushayri's
Epistle on Sufism* (Garnet, 2007; `knysh2007_epistle`, access: owned), to the headings of our Arabic *Risāla*
(`corpus/sufi_manuals/0465IbnHawazinQushayri.RisalaQushayriyya`, a typed text). Stored: Knysh's contents
headings and page numbers only; the translation is copyrighted and not in the repo. Built from a local copy by
`pipeline/verify/build_knysh_concordance.py` (method in its header).

Result: 153 headings matched, 5 weak but correct on inspection (Bishr al-Ḥāfī, Aḥmad b. Khaḍrawayh,
*al-wilāya* p. 268, *al-safar*, *ḥifẓ qulūb al-mashāyikh*), 6 gaps: Knysh's "Chapter 3" title (no Arabic unit),
al-Zaqqāq and Ibn Khafīf (absent from our Arabic edition), Bunān al-Ḥammāl (heading present but not marked as a
heading in our text), *al-rajāʾ* and *al-ḥasad* (to locate by hand).

Chapters central to our discussions: Friendship with God (*wilāya*) p. 268; Miracles of God's friends (*karāmāt
al-awliyāʾ*) p. 357; Divine oneness (*tawḥīd*) p. 306; Gnosis p. 319; Love p. 325.
Use: to read a passage of the Arabic in English, find its heading row and open Knysh at the page given.
It confirms which passage it is and its sense; the Arabic, being typed, needs no OCR check.

# Mathnawi library v6 (rebuilt 2026-09-25)

This zip replaces the lost v6 download. Everything in it was regenerated by script from ganjoor-data commit a64968e78425b2e8c7904fbdf5289fba8251a757, the same commit as the original. Nothing was retyped. Save it on the iPad together with library_v7_addendum_2026-09-25.zip and the six Konya image packs.

## Contents
- mathnawi/mathnawi_book1-6.tsv: exact copies of the project files (Nicholson-numbered).
- mathnawi/full/mathnawi_book1-6.jsonl: full records for 25,637 couplets.
  - Step 1: ingest_full.py, with the three frame maps (Book 1 §§42-96).
  - Step 2: apply_nicholson.py puts in the Nicholson IDs from the TSVs.
  - Every couplet's hemistichs matched the TSVs exactly, and the counts match mathnawi_summary.json.
- mathnawi/reports/: per-book reports (section checksums, warnings, ignored source keys).
- works/data/: Divan-i Shams, Fihi ma fihi, Majalis-i sab'a, Baha Walad's Ma'arif (Part One) and Sultan Walad's Waladnama. Built by works/ingest_works.py, with counts identical to the original works_summary.json.

## How this differs from the original v6
- The JSONL layout is a rebuild. Its concordance entry for Nicholson reads "aligned_unverified" (or "interpolation_candidate" for Ganjoor-only couplets). The original's exact field wording may have differed.
- Not included, because they were produced by reading the Konya images and cannot be regenerated by script:
  - the full couplet-by-couplet Konya tables (Book One, 4,013 rows)
  - variant_sites_konya.tsv (Books 2-6, 1,211 rows)
  - the masnavi.net witness text
  - Nicholson's English column

  Their summaries still live in the project's mathnawi/ folder:
  - konya_book1_differences.tsv
  - book1_variants_konya_not_ganjoor.tsv
  - books2-6_konya_not_ganjoor.tsv
  - collation_pilot.tsv
  - pilot_hadith_check.json

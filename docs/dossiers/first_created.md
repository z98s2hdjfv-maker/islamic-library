## 2. The four answers
| Answer | Who | Where | Standing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Water.** "His Throne was on the water, and He had created nothing before the water"; the heaven was raised from its smoke and the earth from its drying | Ibn ʿAbbas, Ibn Masʿud and other Companions, through al-Suddi | al-Tabari, Tafsir 1:461, `urn:openiti:0310Tabari.JamicBayan.Shamela0007798-ara1:p01574` | a Companions' report in al-Tabari; its chain was not graded in the case |
| **The Pen.** "The first thing Allah created was the Pen; He said to it: write" | the hadith of ʿUbada ibn al-Samit | Abu Dawud 4700; al-Tirmidhi (printed 3319, `urn:hadith:0279Tirmidhi.Sunan:p08820`: "fair and strange"; and 2155 in the book of the decree: "strange") | sound or fair; al-Suyuti calls it sound (nur dossier) |
| **The dust (habaʾ), in which the Muhammadan reality first receives the light** | Ibn ʿArabi | Futuhat 1:118-119, `urn:shamela:ibnarabi.futuhat.arabiyya:r00119`, `r00120` | his unveiling and the philosophers' prime matter; not a hadith |
| **One thing under four names:** "my spirit, my light, the Pen, the Intellect" | Sirr al-asrar | `urn:shamela:jilani.sirrasrar:r00009` | attribution to al-Jilani doubtful; quoted as hadith with no chain |

"The first thing Allah created was my light" is in no hadith collection in the library. Al-ʿAjluni and al-Qastallani attribute it to ʿAbd al-Razzaq "with his chain" and give none (`urn:openiti:1162IbnMuammadAbuFidaCajluni.KashfKhafa.JK000583-ara1:p00891`). "Allah was, and nothing was besides Him; His Throne was on the water" is in al-Bukhari (printed 3191).

## 3. The Malakut
- **Al-Ghazali:** the eye is two eyes; the inner belongs to "the world of the Malakut", beside whose wonders the world of witnessing is small (Mishkat p. 130, `urn:openiti:0505Ghazali.MishkatAnwar.JK009401-ara1:p00011`). "The heart is of the Malakut by its original nature" (`urn:shamela:ghazali.arbain:r00018`).
- **Ibn ʿArabi:** the ether is only the sphere of fire (Futuhat 1:121, `r00122`); the water is the root of life (`r00134`, `r00981`).
- **Rumi:** the world of the Command has no direction (Mathnawi 4:3692-3693); the soul is "in the placeless" (1:1581). See the Rumi dossier.
- **The ether as an Islamic teaching:** no seat says so. It is Said Nursi's reading as the lecture reports him; his works are not in the library.
